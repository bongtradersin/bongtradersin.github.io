Use of the pilot

Terms of Service

Effective date: 7 October 2026. FlowMacro-8 is a personal macro research pilot. This website describes the application and its data practices; it does not provide public access to the private engine.

Purpose and scope

The tool organizes economic observations, selected prediction-market information and conditional explanations for eight markets. It does not perform chart analysis, place trades, manage investments or promise investment performance.

Research limitations

Data can be delayed, revised, unavailable or incomplete. A prediction-contract price is an observed market indication with a specified basis; it is not a guaranteed or calibrated event probability. A change in a funding stock or asset price is not proof of an asset inflow. Review source definitions, timestamps, settlement rules and missing evidence before using a report.

Outputs are research material, not personalized financial advice or a recommendation to buy or sell. The operator remains responsible for their analysis, publication and trading decisions.

Account authorization

Use only Google accounts, files and credentials you are authorized to access. Account authorization is revocable. Read the Privacy Policy for storage, retention and deletion information. Do not put API keys or OAuth tokens into public repositories or pages.

Availability and third-party sources

This development pilot may change or stop. Uninterrupted access, complete market coverage, accurate predictions and automatic daily delivery are not promised. Source providers may impose usage limits and their own terms. Respect their attribution and reuse requirements when publishing source material.
