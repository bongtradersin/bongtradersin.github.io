Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026. This policy describes the current FlowMacro-8 personal research pilot and this public information website.

Google data and its purpose

With the owner's Google authorization, the worker reads project-file metadata and configured spreadsheet values, stores research evidence, data packets, reports and history snapshots in the configured Google Drive folders, and updates the project's control spreadsheet. These operations support collection, report delivery, prior-snapshot comparison and history retention.

The current OAuth permissions may permit broader account access than the project destinations. The worker checks its configured project folder and spreadsheet identities before publication. It is not designed to scan unrelated Google Drive files.

Credentials and storage

OAuth credentials and API keys are configured as private Railway service variables. They are not placed in the public website or source repository. The worker uses temporary runtime storage while collecting data; durable research archives are stored in the owner's Google Drive and recent/control data in Google Sheets.

Service providers and manual analysis

GitHub hosts source code and this information website. Railway executes the research worker; Google provides authorization and Drive/Sheets storage. Public economic and prediction-market APIs supply research data. Each provider processes information needed for its service under its own terms and privacy policies.

The worker currently does not automatically call an AI model API. When the owner chooses to analyze or share a report through ChatGPT or another service, the chosen content is processed by that service. This website does not publish private research archives.

Use and sharing

Google account data is used for the research storage and delivery functions described here. The current application does not implement advertising, sale of Google account data, or automatic sharing of private Google files with other users. This website has no application analytics, account form or tracking scripts installed; GitHub hosting infrastructure may process access logs or other operational information.

FlowMacro-8's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Archived evidence, packets and reports are retained until the owner deletes them. Monthly observation snapshots preserve history when old rows leave the recent Sheet view. Removing a recent row does not delete its archived history. Revoking account authorization stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete existing files.

The owner can revoke access through Google Account's third-party connections settings, remove Railway credentials, and delete project files from Drive and Sheets. For a data or deletion question, contact the support email below. The pilot does not currently offer an automated deletion portal.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change. For privacy and support questions, email bongtradersin@gmail.com.
