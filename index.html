Macro research · Eight markets

Understand the forces
behind market expectations.

FlowMacro-8 is a personal research tool that collects macroeconomic data, monitors selected prediction-market contracts, and explains possible effects on eight markets.

Current status: development pilot. Data collection and archived reports work. Regional, fiscal, geopolitical and measured-flow coverage remain incomplete. Reports contain conditional explanations and disclosed gaps.

Economic evidence

Interest rates, Treasury yields, broad dollar conditions, funding stocks and selected oil inventory and production data.

Expectations watch

Selected Polymarket and Kalshi contracts, their price indications, rules and comparable snapshot changes.

Preserved history

Private data snapshots and reports in the owner's Google Drive, with a rolling recent view in Google Sheets.

Eight research markets

  • BTC/USDT
  • ETH/USDT
  • BNB/USDT
  • XAU/USD
  • WTI crude oil
  • EUR/USD
  • GBP/USD
  • USD/JPY

Why Google account access is used

The research worker uses the owner's authorization to read and write the configured FlowMacro-8 archive folders and control spreadsheet. These archives and credentials are separate from this public website.

Research, not trade execution

The tool does not place orders or perform technical chart analysis. Funding-stock changes, asset prices and prediction-market trading volume are not treated as proof of money flowing into an asset. Report conclusions require further evidence and review.

This website provides app information only. It has no public sign-in, research dashboard or account access form.
